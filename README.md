## About

I am a B.Tech Computer Science student interested in developing practical systems that combine machine learning with reliable software. My projects include smart-home energy optimization, medical-device cybersecurity, molecular simulation, deepfake analysis, and satellite-network optimization.

I enjoy working across the stack—from data preparation and model experiments to APIs, dashboards, deployment, and device integration.
